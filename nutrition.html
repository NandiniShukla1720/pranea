🌰 Seed Cycling — Tracked

Seed cycling is a food-based practice of rotating specific seeds across your cycle to support estrogen dominance in the first half and progesterone support in the second — the lignans in flax and sesame are thought to help modulate estrogen metabolism, while the zinc in pumpkin and selenium in sunflower seeds support progesterone production. Evidence is still emerging, but the seeds themselves are nutrient-dense regardless.

Phase 1 · Days 1–14 (Menstrual + Follicular)1 tbsp Flaxseed + 1 tbsp Pumpkin Seed daily
Phase 2 · Days 15–28 (Ovulatory + Luteal)1 tbsp Sesame Seed + 1 tbsp Sunflower Seed daily
