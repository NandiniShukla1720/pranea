# PRANEA 🌸

"Rooted in science, made for her."

PRANEA is a women's health, fitness, and cycle-literacy platform. It blends everyday Indian wellness wisdom with the biology behind it, translating hormone science into something practical: what to eat, how to move, and what to expect from your own body, phase by phase.

The goal was to build women's-health content grounded in real physiology rather than trend-driven advice, and shaped around Indian food, climate, and culture instead of imported wellness templates.

## What's Inside

**🍛 Nutrition & Diet**
An interactive meal-balance checker, a searchable library of 100 macro-friendly Indian recipes (full ingredients and method, not just names), a clickable hormone-phase eating wheel with sample daily meal plans, a seed-cycling tracker, and a season-and-region guide covering India's food diversity.

**🧘🏽‍♀️ Fitness & Exercise**
An illustrated, 14-pose yoga asana library filterable by cycle phase, with a note on exactly how each pose supports the female body — plus strength and recovery routines explained through a hormonal lens.

**🌙 Mental Health & Wellness**
Journaling prompts, a mood check-in tool, a guided breathing exercise with box-breathing and relaxing modes, a sleep-hygiene checklist, and a 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique.

**✨ Skin & Beauty Care**
A phase-by-phase breakdown of how skin changes across the menstrual cycle — what to expect and what to do about it, backed by the underlying hormone science.

**🌕 Cycle & Body Literacy**
A live, interactive cycle wheel, a visual estrogen/progesterone curve across the month, cycle trivia, and a full calculator suite (BMI, TDEE, macros, ovulation, hydration) with metric/imperial support.

**📝 Blog**
Original long-form articles on cycle science, nutrition, movement, and skin — written to explain the *why* behind the advice.

## Built With

Plain **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript** — no frameworks, no build tools. Every calculator, tracker, and interactive tool runs entirely in the browser.

## Why This Exists

Most women's-health content online is either overly clinical or built on trends with no real science behind them. PRANEA is an attempt at something in between — informative enough to trust, warm enough to actually use, and specific enough to reflect how Indian women eat, move, and live.
