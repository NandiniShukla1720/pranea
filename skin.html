The Science, Simply

Why your skin changes at all.

📉

Low Hormones, Low Glow

During your period, both estrogen and progesterone drop to their monthly low — collagen support dips too, which is why skin can look a little tired this week.

📈

Estrogen & Radiance

As estrogen climbs through the follicular phase and peaks at ovulation, it boosts collagen production and skin hydration — the source of that mid-cycle "glow."

🛢️

Progesterone & Oil

In the luteal phase, progesterone (and relatively higher androgens) stimulate the sebaceous glands — this is the biological reason breakouts cluster before your period.
